# Duotone

Duotone is a small personal music app for iPhone and Windows, built and maintained by one student in their free time.

It is free, has no ads, and is not a commercial product. Duotone does not host, sell or distribute music.

## Download

Builds and release notes are on [joaoafonso.vercel.app](https://joaoafonso.vercel.app/#apps).

## Privacy

Your account, library and messages are stored only to make the app work, and are never sold. You can delete your account and its data at any time in **Settings › Delete account**.

Full details: [Privacy & Terms](https://joaoafonso.vercel.app/duotone/privacy).
