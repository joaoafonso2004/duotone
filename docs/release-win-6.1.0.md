Duotone for Windows 6.1.0

New
- Playlist folders, in Playlists: drag a playlist onto a folder, or onto another playlist to make a folder or merge the two.
- Shuffle again and select several songs in the queue (Ctrl or Shift+click; Esc lets go).
- Share an album with a friend from the album's menu.
- Export your library in Settings: Liked Songs, playlists and albums, as JSON or CSV.
- ListenBrainz scrobbling in Settings: paste your token and your listens go straight from your PC. Private listening sends nothing.

Improved
- An artist's songs are in one list, in the order you choose.
- The update notice shows what's new instead of the version title.
- Fewer requests when Duotone opens.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
