Duotone for iPhone 6.2.0

New
- Opening a playlist, folder, mix or artist grows the page out of the cover you tapped, and the cover lands at the top of the page. Back shrinks it into the same spot.
- QR code in Send to friends: tap the QR next to the playlist, and a friend's iPhone camera opens it in Duotone. It's drawn on your iPhone.
- Lists on Home (mixes and See all) now have Play and Shuffle.

Improved
- Every round play button is the metal one: the song of the moment, artist pages, playlists, the Daily mix and songs in chats. It follows Customise → Play button.
- "Playing from" at the top of the player now says where the song came from: profiles, Home shelves, search, a friend's song, Downloads, stats, Year in review, You two, albums and "Play now" in menus. In a Jam it says Jam and opens it.
- Songs on profiles and in chats open the same menu as everywhere else, and holding a song opens it. "Remove from profile" is there for your song of the moment.

Fixed
- Swiping a message to reply no longer loses to the chat scrolling.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
