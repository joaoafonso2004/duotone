Playlists and artists now open without the page changing under you, and going back no longer blows up the cover.

Duotone for iPhone 7.2.7

Improved
- Opening a playlist shows its cover, name and buttons right away. Only the songs wait, in their place.
- Opening an artist shows the songs once, already complete and in the right order. Before, the list changed several times while it loaded.
- Coming back to an artist's page no longer reloads it.

Fixed
- Swiping back from a playlist or an artist: the cover shrinks from where it is on the page to its card, instead of growing to the size of the screen.

Minimum version: 7.1.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/ios-v7.2.6...ios-v7.2.7
