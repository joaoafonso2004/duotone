The real iOS 26 tab bar, glass opacity you can set, and a tidier mini player.

Duotone for iPhone 7.3.2

New
- Liquid Glass: the tabs are now the system tab bar, so you can hold the selected tab and drag the glass lens from tab to tab.
- Customise: a Glass opacity slider under Liquid Glass, from clear glass to a dark capsule.
- Up next: an X on the "Back to …" row hides it.

Improved
- Liquid Glass: the mini player capsule is now smaller than the tab bar, and its artwork has rounded corners and sits centred.

Fixed
- The mini player artwork had almost square corners; they are rounded again.

Minimum version: 7.1.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/ios-v7.3.1...ios-v7.3.2
