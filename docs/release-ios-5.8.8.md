Duotone for iPhone 5.8.8

New
- Profiles of people who aren't your friends show their listening stats, never what they listen to. You can hide yours in Edit profile › Privacy.
- Spotify sign-in works with any account.
- Updates are also checked when you come back to the app. Some future updates may be required.

Improved
- Without internet, Home, your profile and Social stay on screen, with a "No internet connection" bar. A message you were typing is kept.
- Playing a song from a chat plays the rest of the chat after it, shown as "From chat with …". Tapping a song in a chat opens the usual song menu.
- "Import a playlist" in Playlists takes links from anywhere.
- Artist pages show more albums.
- Fewer requests when you return to the app: stats and per-song settings load only what changed.

Fixed
- Song covers in chats now show.
- A song titled with another artist's name is credited to the right artist.

Please update: older versions will stop being supported.
