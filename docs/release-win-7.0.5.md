The cover that flies into Now Playing turns from flat to 3D on the way, lands without a blink, and already leans toward your mouse.

Duotone for Windows 7.0.5

Improved
- Opening Now Playing, the cover leaves the player bar flat, like the thumbnail, and becomes the 3D cover halfway there. Closing, it flattens back before it reaches the bar.
- The cover lands at the exact size of the one on the page, so it no longer arrives small and then jumps bigger.
- With the 3D cover, it arrives already tilted toward the mouse, instead of resting and then snapping when the mouse moves.
- Without the 3D cover, the flat cover's corners now match the thumbnail in the player bar.

Minimum version: 7.0.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/win-v7.0.4...win-v7.0.5
