Duotone for iPhone 5.9.3

New
- Choose how much space saved songs can use, from 10 MB to unlimited, in Settings › Storage.
- Download the IPA straight from the update notice, then open it in SideStore or AltStore.

Fixed
- On your profile, your name in the top bar no longer runs into the buttons when you scroll.

Improved
- Much lighter on the server: what you listen to is sent in one small batch every few minutes, and friends' online status no longer writes to the database. This keeps Duotone free for everyone.
- Friends' status and what they're playing arrive live.
- The like button in the player answers instantly.

Minimum version: 5.9.3
