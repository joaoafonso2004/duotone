Duotone for iPhone 6.0.1

New
- Save albums to your library. They live in an Albums folder at the top of Playlists: tap ♡ on an album, or hold it and choose Save album.
- Download a whole playlist at once from its ⋯ menu. On mobile data it waits for Wi-Fi, unless you tap Use mobile data.
- Report a problem in Settings: tell the developer what happened, with the playback report if you want.

Improved
- If sign-ups are closed for a while, Duotone says so clearly.

Fixed
- Some imported songs played the music video instead of the song, which put the lyrics out of time.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
