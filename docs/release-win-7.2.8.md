Find any setting in seconds, a cleaner Up next, and a shuffle that goes through everything.

Duotone for Windows 7.2.8

New
- Search in Settings: type a name or what it does ("fullscreen", "tray"), and it opens the right section and lights up the setting.

Improved
- Up next shows just the queue. The Now playing row above it repeated the player bar.
- Shuffle goes through the whole list before repeating, even when you shuffle again or restart Duotone.

Fixed
- The glow under the selected tab (Up next, Radio) and the dark shadow under a friend's cover are gone.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
