A fuller About this song, a solid top bar, and a mini player that lands cleanly.

Duotone for iPhone 7.3.3

New
- About this song now shows what the Windows app shows: the artist (photo, fans, a way to their page and the favourite star), how often you play them, their latest release, your most played of theirs, who fans also like, and the chorus.

Improved
- The bar at the top of Home, Liked Songs, Artists and Playlists is now solid when you scroll, instead of showing the list through it.
- Liquid Glass: the glass no longer looks grey and muddy, and Glass opacity now really goes from clear to dark.

Fixed
- Closing the player no longer makes the mini player artwork flash black after it lands.
- With Liquid Glass, the artwork's corners round off as it shrinks into the mini player. Without it, the mini player artwork has its almost square corners back.

Minimum version: 7.3.3
