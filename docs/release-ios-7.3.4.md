Liquid Glass you can see through, and pages that open without the one behind showing.

Duotone for iPhone 7.3.4

Improved
- Liquid Glass: the glass now blurs what's behind it while staying see-through. At 0% Glass opacity it's clear glass with a soft blur; higher values darken it.
- Liquid Glass: swiping the mini player away to close it takes its glass with it.

Fixed
- Opening an artist or a playlist no longer shows the previous page through the new one while it opens.
- Starting to swipe back on a playlist or artist and letting go no longer makes the screen flicker.

Minimum version: 7.3.3
