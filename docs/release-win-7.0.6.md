Clicking a lyric line with the 3D cover jumps there again, and switches, menus and shelves feel more at home on Windows.

Duotone for Windows 7.0.6

Improved
- The Albums folder opens up on hover, and the tiles of a playlist mosaic lift apart.
- One metal switch everywhere: the Jam, Settings and the EQ presets. Changing section in Settings slides the new card in.
- Shelf arrows and dragging stop at the start of a card, and so do the trackpad and the wheel.
- Recommendations and Edit profile open in the PC's own dialog.
- The song of the moment on a profile has the metal play button, and its menu has "Remove from profile" on your own profile.
- "Play now" from a profile or a chat menu shows where the song is playing from.

Fixed
- With the 3D cover turned to the lyrics, clicking a line jumps there instead of doing nothing most of the time.
- Closing a menu opened with the mouse no longer leaves a keyboard outline on the card.
- The Spotify import choices respond to the mouse.

Minimum version: 7.0.0
