Playlist folders: keep your playlists together, and move, rename or undo a folder whenever you like.
The queue: shuffle what's next again, or select several songs to play next or remove, with Undo.
Radio never gives you the same queue twice for the same song.
Your drawer works like the Home Screen: hold a tile to lift it, drag it anywhere, and pull its corner to resize it.

Duotone for iPhone 6.1.0

New
- Playlist folders, in Playlists.
- Shuffle again and select several songs in the queue.
- Share an album with a friend from the album's menu.
- Export your library in Settings: Liked Songs, playlists and albums, as JSON or CSV.
- ListenBrainz scrobbling in Settings: paste your token and your listens go straight from your phone. Private listening sends nothing.

Improved
- The drawer: drag tiles to move them, pull the corner to resize, tap an empty spot to finish. Only your spaces sit on top, friends who aren't playing open their profile, and the now-playing cover has no black bars.
- In edit mode, the space name shows in your language.
- An artist's songs are in one list, in the order you choose.
- Liked Songs and playlists scroll faster, and the app keeps its data in faster storage.
- The keyboard in conversations moves smoothly with the text.
- Closing the player no longer zooms out the app behind it.

Fixed
- The menus in the queue and Radio now open next to the button, not at the top of the screen.
- The Home keeps its rounded corners when the drawer is open.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
