Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, new app icons in Customise, word-by-word lyrics and About this song with BPM and Camelot key.
Albums open from their cover too, swiping right shrinks a page back into its card, and holding a song or card lifts it for its menu.

Duotone for iPhone 7.1.0

New
- Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island when a Jam, sleep timer or AutoMix transition is active.
- App icon in Customise: choose between the classic icon and Metal, Two-tone, Light or Night.
- About this song in any song's menu (in the player and in lists), showing your plays along with the tempo and Camelot key measured by AutoMix.
- Word-by-word lyrics on songs that include per-word timing.

Improved
- Eclipse transition on song change in the player and mini player, shifting the app's tint together with the cover.
- Flowing lyrics move line by line with a spring (Customise › Lyrics › Flowing lyrics, on by default).
- Swipe right anywhere on a page opened from a card to shrink it back into its spot, with the previous screen behind it.
- Albums now open as a full page out of their cover, songs cascade in when the cover lands, and opening and going back respond immediately.
- Home search expands from the search bar, recent searches arrive one by one, and Cancel brings Home back where you left it.
- Long-pressing a song or card (Playlists, Home shelves, Saved albums) lifts it above the blur and opens its menu from it.
- Numbers in Listening stats, You two, Year in review and playlist counts roll like an odometer.
- Empty views in Downloads, Saved albums, empty playlists and stats point you where to go.
- On slow connections, the opening logo breathes until your session is ready.
- "Playing from" also shows for "Play now" in menus, Radio seeds, profiles and chats.

Fixed
- Enhanced lyrics no longer display raw word timestamps in the text.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
