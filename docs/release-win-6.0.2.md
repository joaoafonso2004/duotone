Duotone for Windows 6.0.2

New
- Duotone in French: Settings › Appearance › Language. English stays the default, and Duotone reloads to switch.
- Start Radio in a song's menu (right-click, or the menu in the player bar): it plays the song and fills Up next with music like it.
- Show what your iPhone is playing, in Settings › Windows: turn it off and your iPhone's music stops showing up here.

Improved
- The equaliser is now separate on PC and iPhone: each song's EQ and speed, and your default EQ, stay on the platform where you set them. Your presets are still the same on both.

Minimum version: 6.0.1

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/win-v6.0.1...win-v6.0.2
