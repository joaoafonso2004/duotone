Choose your own playlist cover, and see what your friends are playing without the delay.

Duotone for Windows 7.2.5

New
- Playlist covers: right-click one of your playlists and choose "Choose cover…" to use any picture. "Change cover…" and "Remove cover" are in the same menu. The cover shows everywhere, on your iPhone too.

Fixed
- What a friend is playing shows up right away again. After a dropped connection (the PC waking from sleep, a network blip), friends could stay on "Online" or an old song for minutes.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
