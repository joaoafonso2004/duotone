Download songs to listen offline, a full screen that follows the music, and the mini player is back and tidier.

Duotone for Windows 7.0.7

New
- Download a song or a whole playlist from its menu. Without a connection, the queue plays what is on the computer.
- Ambient full screen: when music is playing and you leave Duotone alone for a minute, it fills its screen. Move the mouse over it to come back. It never takes the keyboard from the window you are working in. You can turn it off in Settings › Windows.
- Clean mode (F11) shows the lyrics next to the cover, the cover reacts to the music, and changing song is an eclipse in the colour of the next cover.

Improved
- The mini player takes the colour of the cover, always shows the artist, keeps the window buttons in a strip at the top, and shows the time at both ends of the bar. Hover Next to see what comes up.
- The queue card and About in the right panel are cleaner: plain tabs, a Now playing block with its progress, how long the queue lasts, and About in two columns when there is room.
- More places say where a song is playing from: right-click menus on shelves, search, Liked Songs, mixes, artists, albums and playlists, and the songs you play from stats, Year in review and chats.

Fixed
- The mini player opens again from the player bar and the tray.

Minimum version: 7.0.0
