Duotone for iPhone 6.0.2

New
- Duotone in French: Settings › Appearance › Language. English stays the default, and the new language shows the next time you open Duotone.
- Download Liked Songs in Settings › Storage: all of them, and the ones you like later. On mobile data it waits for Wi-Fi.
- Start Radio in a song's menu: it plays the song and fills Up next with music like it.
- Show what your PC is playing, in Settings › General: turn it off and your PC's music stops showing up here.
- More buttons for below the player, in Settings › Customise › Buttons below: Sleep timer, Start Radio, Add to playlist, Download and Car mode. The sleep timer shows the time left.

Improved
- The buttons below the player fit their names on one line.
- The equaliser is now separate on iPhone and PC: each song's EQ and speed, and your default EQ, stay on the platform where you set them. Your presets are still the same on both.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
