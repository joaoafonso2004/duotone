Your own drawer on Home: swipe from the left edge, or tap the new button next to Home.
Fill it with artists, playlists, friends, controls and shortcuts. Hold any tile to edit your drawer.
Scenes change several settings in one tap, like Night: private listening and a 30 min sleep timer. Tap it again and everything goes back.
It starts with what you play most, so it's ready the first time you open it.

Duotone for iPhone 6.0.4

New
- The drawer on Home: spaces (Everyday and up to four more), and tiles for what's playing, your PC, artists, playlists, albums, friends, the sleep timer, private listening, speed, shuffle, radio and the places you go to most.
- Scenes: Night, Focus, Gym and Drive are ready to add, and the scene editor makes your own.
