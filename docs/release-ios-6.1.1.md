Poster: a photo of the artist fills Now Playing, a different one for each song.
Black and white with grain, or in colour, fading into the photo's own colour at the top and bottom.
Pick it in Customise, or the new Poster theme does it all in one tap.
Controls, in Customise: keep the heart and the play ring white, with the colour only in the background.

Duotone for iPhone 6.1.1

New
- Poster, in Customise → Artwork: a photo of the artist behind the top, the title and the controls. Each song gets its own photo while the artist has photos; without them, the artwork. The lyrics still turn in the square.
- Poster image: artist photos or the artwork. Black and white: the Poster with grain, like a printed photo.
- The Poster theme: artist photos in black and white, "Playing from", the ring play button and white controls in one tap.
- Controls, in Customise → Now Playing: Accent colour or White for the heart, the play ring, the dots, shuffle and repeat.

Improved
- The preview in Customise shows the progress bar in white, as in Now Playing.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
