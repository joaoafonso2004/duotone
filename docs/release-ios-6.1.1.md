Poster: the artwork fills Now Playing, behind the title and the controls.
At the top and bottom it fades into the artwork's own colour, not black.
Pick it in Customise, as an artwork style or with the new Poster theme.
Controls, in Customise: keep the heart and the play ring white, with the colour only in the background.

Duotone for iPhone 6.1.1

New
- Poster, in Customise → Artwork: the artwork behind the top, the title and the controls, fading into its own colour. The lyrics still turn in the square.
- The Poster theme: the Poster artwork, "Playing from", the ring play button and white controls in one tap.
- Controls, in Customise → Now Playing: Accent colour or White for the heart, the play ring, the dots, shuffle and repeat.

Improved
- The preview in Customise shows the progress bar in white, as in Now Playing.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
