See your look change as you customise, try Liquid Glass, and find any setting in seconds.

Duotone for iPhone 7.2.8

New
- Customise shows the app live: Now Playing next to Home or a playlist, changing as you adjust. "Next song" previews the colours of what's coming up.
- Liquid Glass: on iOS 26, turn it on in Customise for the tab bar, the mini player and the player's buttons in glass. Off by default.
- Background follows the song: the artwork's colour washes the top of every page and changes with the music. Off by default, in Customise.
- Search in Settings: type a name or what it does, and it opens the right section and lights up the setting.

Improved
- Settings show the one or two options you use most in each section. "More" opens the rest right there.
- Shuffle goes through the whole list before repeating, even when you shuffle again or reopen the app.

Fixed
- Opening a playlist: the cover lands in its place, without jumping up when the songs arrive.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
