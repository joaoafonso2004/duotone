Duotone for iPhone 6.1.2

New
- The metal play button, as on the PC: a brushed-metal disc with a ring of light that spins up when you press play and brakes like a turntable when you pause. It's the new default in Now Playing and the mini player; pick another style in Customise → Play button.
- "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen, and "Reset password" in Settings: we email you a code, and you choose the new password right there.

Improved
- Spotify playlist links with more than 100 songs now bring the rest too, up to 1000.
- Fewer requests in the background: the heart no longer asks the server about every song you play.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
