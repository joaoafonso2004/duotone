Choose your own playlist cover, signing in works again, and a smoother Eclipse skip.

Duotone for iPhone 7.2.5

New
- Choose your own playlist cover: open a playlist's menu and pick "Choose cover…" to use any photo. "Change cover…" and "Remove cover" are there once it has one. Friends who can see the playlist see the cover too.

Fixed
- Signing in works again. On 7.2.3, signing in failed with "Cannot read property 'removeItem' of undefined", and you could be signed out when your session renewed.
- The Eclipse skip animation passes smoothly again on the 3D cover.

Minimum version: 7.1.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/ios-v7.2.3...ios-v7.2.5
