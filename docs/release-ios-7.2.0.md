Save your Customise looks as presets, and pages now open the moment you tap them.
Artists open straight into their photo, the original app icon can be chosen again, and long-press menus never cover the song.

Duotone for iPhone 7.2.0

New
- Presets in Customise: save the look you have now with a name, apply it with a tap, and hold it to rename, update or delete it.

Improved
- Playlists, albums and artists open right away: the page appears under the cover while it is still flying, with no wait on a black screen.
- Opening an artist turns their circle straight into the full-width photo, with no swap at the end.
- Swiping back shrinks the page into its card's cover instead of a dark box.
- Long-pressing a song lifts it with rounded corners, and its menu opens below or above it, never on top.
- Albums save as a playlist from a + button in the top corner.
- Flowing lyrics are now off by default; turn them on in Customise › Lyrics.

Fixed
- The Original app icon can be selected again after choosing another one.
- The back arrow no longer shows a page slide behind the cover.
- The 3D cover's sides no longer switch to the next song before the eclipse covers the front.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
