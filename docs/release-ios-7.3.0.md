Liquid Glass done properly, presets you can edit again, and playlists that open without a jump.

Duotone for iPhone 7.3.0

Improved
- Liquid Glass, redone: the tabs and the mini player float in two separate glass capsules, as in iOS 26, and a highlight follows your finger as you swipe between tabs. Turn it on in Customise.

Fixed
- Holding a preset in Customise no longer closes the app. Rename, Update to current look and Delete are back.
- Opening a playlist: the cover lands in its place, without landing low and jumping up.

Minimum version: 7.1.0
