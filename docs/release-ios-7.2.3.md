Faster skips, and a way to switch Spotify accounts when importing.

Duotone for iPhone 7.2.3

Improved
- Skipping to a song that hasn't finished preloading (several skips in a row, or right after a song starts) gets it playing sooner: the download now fetches its pieces in parallel.

Fixed
- Importing from your Spotify account now shows which account you're signed in with, and "Use another account" lets you sign out and log in with a different one.

Minimum version: 7.1.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/ios-v7.2.2...ios-v7.2.3
