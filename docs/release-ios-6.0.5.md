The drawer opens with a swipe anywhere on Home, not just from the edge.
It takes on the colours of what's playing, and no longer flickers when it opens.
Hold any tile to edit your drawer. The Edit button is gone.
New drawers start with the Night scene, and yours gets it once if it has no scene yet.

Duotone for iPhone 6.0.5

Improved
- Swipe right anywhere on Home to open your drawer. Swiping left still takes you to Songs, and the shelves keep their own scrolling.
- The drawer's background follows the colours of the song that's playing, and the tiles let them through.
- Holding a tile is now the way to edit; Done appears while you edit. An empty space shows Add.
- The drawer starts with the Night scene.

Fixed
- The tiles no longer blink when the drawer opens: they appear in order as it slides in.

Minimum version: 6.0.1
