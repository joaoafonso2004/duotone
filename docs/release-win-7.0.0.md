A new Duotone for Windows: lighter, and smoother everywhere.
If you were on 6.2.0, your account and your settings come along by themselves.

Duotone for Windows 7.0.0

New
- A new engine under the hood: Duotone uses less memory and less processor while it plays.
- A new Social page: see what a friend is playing in a panel beside your chat, with what comes up next and Listen along. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower.
- Smoother everywhere: dialogs, menus, notices, panels and the player bar now slide in and out instead of popping.

Fixed
- Friends in a Jam show the song they're listening to again, instead of just "Online".

Changed
- If you use Spotify, sign in to Spotify again once.
- Discord no longer shows what you're listening to.
- Keyboard shortcuts that worked outside Duotone are gone. Your keyboard's media keys still work.

Minimum version: 7.0.0

Full changelog: https://github.com/joaoafonso2004/duotone/compare/win-v6.2.0...win-v7.0.0
